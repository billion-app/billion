CERTIFIED LIST OF CANDIDATES FOR THE
NOVEMBER 3, 2026, GENERAL ELECTION
OFFICE OF THE SECRETARY OF STATE
STATE OF CALIFORNIA
I, Shirley N. Weber, Ph.D., Secretary of State of the State of California, do hereby certify
That the following list contains the name, office sought, and, if applicable, the
ballot designation and party preference of each person nominated at the June 2, 2026,
Primary Election, and the names of the justices of the Supreme Court and of the courts of
appeal who are entitled to receive votes at the General Election to be held on Tuesday,
November 3, 2026.
Dated at Sacramento, California, this 27th day of August, 2026.
Shirley N. Weber, Ph.D.
